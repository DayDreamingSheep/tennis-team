Pocket Tennis Teamv0.2 Preview

A whole pro team behind every player

Talk to your Head Coach after practice. Six AI specialists remember everything, spot your patterns, and keep a small weekly plan where every item tells you why. A Claude plugin: voice-first, evidence-based, private by default.

▶ Try the live demo View on GitHub
The demo runs in your browser with a sample player; no install or account needed.

Why it exists

Club and junior players hear advice from lessons, YouTube, teammates and parents, and almost none of it is remembered or turned into a plan. A pro has a coach, an analyst, a fitness coach and someone tracking every match. Pocket Tennis Team gives an everyday player the same structure.

🎙 Just talk

Hands-free conversation with a natural voice. Messy voice notes become a clean training diary.

📋 A plan with reasons

A 2-minute interview builds your plan on screen. Every focus shows who decided it and the words of yours it came from.

🩹 Health first

Pain is always answered first, junior growth guardrails are built in, and overload warnings come before you enter a tournament.

How it works

  1. Install the plugin in Claude (desktop app or Claude Code). When Claude starts, the app opens in your browser.
  2. Pick a folder; everything you say stays there as plain files you own.
  3. Each message gets a fast reply from your Head Coach, then the team updates your journal, plan and calendar.

For Claude users:

/plugin marketplace add DayDreamingSheep/tennis-team
/plugin install tennis-team@tennis-team
