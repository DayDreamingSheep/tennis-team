Decision gates

Four checkpoints where the evidence decides the next step, not enthusiasm.

G1 · Preview shipped

Mon, Oct 26
  • Install from GitHub works on 2 Macs
  • Live demo runs without Claude
  • README and case study are ready to show
If not: slip one week; never ship a broken first run.

G2 · Beta health

Sun, Nov 22
  • 5+ testers active
  • 60% log a second note within 7 days
  • Average reply under 20 s; no lost notes
If not: interview the drop-outs and fix the first-week experience before v0.3 features.

G3 · v0.3 quality

Mon, Jan 18
  • Persona retest 4.5+ everywhere
  • Coach starts speaking within 5 s
  • Ready for directory review
If not: keep polishing; directory submission waits.

G4 · Profit decision

Mon, Mar 1
  • 40% of testers still active at week 8
  • 3 of 10 interviewees would pay a monthly fee (test about $8–15)
  • One clear segment: adult players, parents, or coaches
  • Per-user AI cost fits under the price
If no-go: keep it as an open portfolio project with small releases. Nothing is wasted.
