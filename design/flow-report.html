Flow and structure report · v3 tested · Oct 15, 2026

A whole tennis team, one conversation away

How the Tennis Team plugin is put together and every path a player or parent walks through it: from the first launch, through the coaching interview and daily training, to tournaments and the weekly review.

Status: v0.1 plugin built · v2 and v3 prototypes tested with personas · v0.2 build next Personas: Sam (adult) · Mei, parent of Alex (14) Repo: ~/tennis-team
01

At a glance

The promise, the principles every screen follows, and where each part stands today.

The promise. Someone who has never typed a command starts Claude, the app is already on screen, and within about five minutes they have talked to their coach, watched their plan take shape with reasons, and know their one thing for this week.

The page is the product

Nobody types commands or skill names. Claude Code or Cowork runs behind a local web app that opens on its own.

A coach, not a dashboard

The conversation is the main surface. The mic and text box are always one tap away, and replies are signed by the team member who wrote them.

Every plan item has a Why

Each focus links to the player's own words, the team member who decided it, and a success target. First-week plans are labelled as starters.

Never wait on the AI to move on

Questions, chips and the plan board respond instantly. The slow AI step (20–60 s per run) happens in the background and arrives as a card.

Small, honest load

At most three tennis focuses and one fitness block per week. Top priorities first. One good session is a candidate pattern, not proof.

Health comes first

Tightness means monitor, pain means pause and see a professional, limping mid-match means stop now. Juniors get growth guardrails.

Plugin (public repo)

Skills, workspace templates, the voice server and the launch hook. No personal data. Installs from a GitHub marketplace.

Workspace (private folder)

One folder per player. Notes, patterns, plan, coach evidence, tournaments. Plain files the player owns.

App page (localhost)

Reads plan data instantly, writes onboarding answers and quick actions without AI, and queues AI jobs that never block the mic.

Verified platform facts

  • Plugin hooks/hooks.json with an async SessionStart hook runs in the CLI, the desktop Code tab and Cowork.
  • No install-time hook: the app first opens at the first session after install.
  • Paths available: CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT, CLAUDE_PLUGIN_DATA, CLAUDE_PROJECT_DIR.

Opt-out and backups

  • Welcome: "Don't open this automatically".
  • Settings switch "Open automatically when I start Claude in my tennis folder". Both write no-autoopen.
  • Backup doors: a launcher file in the folder, and /tennis.

Team huddle (hides the AI wait)

Members speak in turn while the job runs: "Strategy Coach: phase 1 is about stopping free points." "Tournament Coach: we ease off the week of Nov 2." A progress bar, then the review.

Plan review

Labelled "Starter plan, adjusted after your first 2 sessions". Each focus has a Why? (reason, the player's quote, who decided), a target, and Looks good / Too much / Change this.

Skipping. Every question can be skipped. "Skip interview" produces a starter plan clearly labelled as generic, and the coach fills gaps from later notes, asking at most one profile question per weekly review.

Daily training loop

Guided questions run in the page with no AI wait between them; one background job does the thinking; every tab updates from the same store.

sequenceDiagram
  autonumber
  actor P as Player
  participant H as Home
  participant T as Guided template (in page)
  participant Q as Job queue
  participant AI as Team (claude -p)
  participant S as Workspace + store
  H->>P: Today strip: Practice, cue "Short swing, deep middle"
  Note over P: plays the session
  H->>P: Chip appears: How did practice go?
  P->>T: Practice, match or lesson?
  P->>T: How did your one thing go? 6 of 10
  P->>T: What cost you the most points? Return errors
  P->>T: Anything sore? A little, left calf
  T->>P: Review screen with Edit links, then Send
  T->>Q: One note
  Q->>AI: Run (mic stays live)
  AI->>S: Entry, patterns, decision, actions, plan data
  AI-->>H: Reply card: calf first, then 6 of 10 feedback, up to 3 next steps
  S-->>H: Calendar Done, Plan "How it's going", Team "Latest" refresh
  P->>H: Ticks a next step (no AI call)

How did it go?

Type · what happened (matches) · your one thing out of 10 · what cost points (chips) · body check. Losses get a different reply than wins.

After my lesson

Which coach · what they told you, in their words · anything to check you understood. Journaled first, then the Coach Analyst sorts principles from ideas to test.

Today's focus

Instant, no AI. Reads the current assignments; after a session is logged, "Next" moves to the following one.

11

Weekly review and assignment decisions

Runs automatically the first time the player opens the app after the plan's week ends. Every assignment gets exactly one decision.

stateDiagram-v2
  [*] --> New: created this week
  New --> Keep: evidence sparse or mixed
  Keep --> Test: promising but felt-only
  Test --> LevelUp: target met in real points
  Keep --> LevelUp: target met repeatably
  Keep --> Simplify: too complex or tiring
  Keep --> Rest: pain or clash
  Rest --> Keep: cleared
  Keep --> Swap: no transfer after a fair trial
  LevelUp --> Keep: new, harder target
  Swap --> [*]
File termPlayer seesRule
newNew this weekSetup or review only
keepKeep goingThe default when evidence is thin
validateTest it in real pointsNo live or pressure evidence yet
progressLevel upTarget met repeatably in live play
reduceSimplifyComplexity or recovery is poor
pauseRest itPain, illness, higher priority
replaceSwap itOnly with a stated reason

Caps: 3 tennis + 1 fitness per week, at least one top priority every week, at most 3 open next steps, and no new technique while the current model is being tested, unless a coach prescribed it directly.

Junior load guardrails

  • Warn at 2+ tournament weekends in a row, or more than 4 singles matches in a day.
  • Warn when planned weekly hours jump by more than 30%, or exceed about one hour per year of age.
  • Suggest rest after back-to-back match days; rest days replace training.

Wellbeing

Signs of distress, burnout, self-harm or disordered eating pause tennis coaching. The coach replies warmly and plainly, encourages talking to a trusted adult, and shows crisis resources. The team never contacts anyone.

Recognition

Browser recognition for the live preview. Optional Whisper download (about 500 MB) for accents and mixed Chinese and English; private and offline.

Correction

Tap any word to fix it; the mic adds to the end. Likely tennis mishearings are fixed and highlighted with Undo.

Speaking

Novelty voices hidden; Premium, Natural and Google voices preferred; choice remembered; Chrome's dropped and stalled speech handled.

Evidence order

1 · Direct

Coaching given to this player (lesson notes, a coach's feedback). Can create principles.

2 · Player

The player's own notes and words. In parent mode, Alex's words outrank parent-reported fields.

3 · Public and general

A coach's public material, then general tennis knowledge. Fills gaps, never silently overrides.

  • Pain ignored; health now leads every reply.
  • Fake history for new users; now empty and seeded from answers.
  • Rest days stacked on training; overload checked only after booking.
