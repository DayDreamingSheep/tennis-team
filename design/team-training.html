Pocket Tennis Team · design proposal · Oct 15, 2026

Training your tennis team

Only two team members need real knowledge: the Head Coach and the Fitness Coach. The rest need a few choices, or nothing at all. Players teach with three things they already have: notes from lessons, videos and links they learn from, and what happened on court.

Teach2 membersLearn from your lessons, videos and body. They hold the knowledge.
Adjust3 membersA few settings, or just say "make my plan more aggressive".
Automatic1 memberRuns on fixed rules you can read. Nothing to set up.

1 · The problem

"Set up & train" on every card asks too much and explains too little

Today all six cards show the same button. A player can't tell where their effort pays off. Training the Progress Coordinator means nothing: it applies rules. Training the Head Coach means a lot, but the page doesn't say what to bring. Parents and low-tech players need to see one obvious place to teach, and know the rest is handled.

2 · The new team map

Three kinds of team member, each with its own button

The Coach Analyst stops being a separate card. It becomes the engine behind "Teach your Head Coach", so players never have to understand the difference.

3 · Three ways to teach

Bring what you already have

Each input has one button on the Teach page, and can also just be said on Home. "Had a lesson with Priya, she said…" goes to the same place.

📝 After a lesson

"Priya said toss more to the right and keep my head up until after contact. Say 'toss right, head up'."

  1. Journal Keeper saves it as a lesson in your diary.
  2. Coach Analyst pulls out the cue, what it's for, and the exact words.
  3. The Head Coach learns it as taught by Priya.
  4. If it's about something you're working on, the new cue replaces the old one this week.

Strongest evidence. A real coach's lesson for you beats everything else.

▶️ A video or link

Paste a YouTube video or channel, an article, a Loom, or a Google Doc.

  1. Coach Analyst studies what's public about it: pages, docs, video titles and descriptions. (Shipped in v0.2.5.)
  2. Study notes list the main ideas, how they fit your plan, and what to watch first.
  3. The Head Coach asks: "What's the one thing you took from it?"
  4. Your takeaway becomes an idea to test next practice.

Public material never overrides your coach. A clash gets flagged for your next lesson.

🎾 What happened on court

"Tried the early split step today: seven of ten returns back deep. It felt rushed on the backhand side."

  1. Journal Keeper logs it with the numbers.
  2. The idea being tested gets a result: worked, partly, or didn't.
  3. Two good sessions with numbers make it proven for you. A clear miss sends it back with a question for your coach.

Court results decide what sticks. This is how the team learns your tennis, not tennis in general.

7 · Keeping Planner and Head Coach in sync

One plan, two owners, no silent disagreements

QuestionOwnerReadsExample
What to work on, and whyHead CoachWhat it knows (taught, proven, testing) and your journal patterns"Returns first: they cost you the most points."
How much, how often, by whenPlannerIntensity, days a week, goal date, upcoming tournaments, health flags"4 days this week, lighter before Sunday's match."
Keep, level up or simplifyProgress CoordinatorThe numbers from your notes, against each focus's target"7/10 twice → level up the target to 8/10."
HealthFitness Coach, always firstBody checks, physio notes, injury history"Heel sore → low impact only until the physio check."

All four write to the same plan file. When the Planner's intensity and the Head Coach's focus pull in different directions, the plan keeps the safer option and shows one line explaining why.

8 · Fitness Coach (new)

Same Teach page pattern, with health inputs instead of tennis ones:

10 · Decisions needed

Three calls before building

Fold the Coach Analyst into the Head Coach?Recommended. Players teach "my coach", and the analyst keeps working behind the scenes, credited in the activity steps.
Rename Strategy Coach to Planner?Recommended. It says what it does: when and how much.
