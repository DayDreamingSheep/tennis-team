# Entry templates

Every entry starts with:

```markdown
## NOTE-YYYY-MM-DD-NN - <short title>

- Date:
- Type: <primary> [/ <secondary>]
- Context: <surface, opponent/partner level, session length, conditions, energy>
```

Then the type-specific body, then the shared footer.

## practice note

### Observations
### What worked
### What broke (and when - early, late, under fatigue, under pressure)
### Interpretation

## match note (includes tournaments)

- Opponent: <style, level, handedness> - Result: <score or unknown>
### Plan vs reality
### Points won by / Points lost by (patterns, not every point)
### Pressure moments (serving/returning at 30-30, break points, tiebreaks)
### Physical / mental state over the match
### Interpretation

## pre-match plan

- Opponent / scouting known:
### Game plan (max 3 patterns)
### Serve plan / Return plan
### Plan B if losing
### Reset cue between points
After the match, link the plan from the match note and score each plan item: used / abandoned / worked / failed.

## lesson (learning experience)

- Coach / source:
### What the coach said (as close to verbatim as possible)
### What I felt / tried
### Interpretation and open questions

## video review

- Source: <own footage, coach video, pro footage> - Link/file:
### What the video shows
### Comparison with what I felt
### Interpretation

## Shared footer

```markdown
### Priority and cycle connection
### Limitations
### Assignment decision
### Next actions (1-3)
```

## body check

- Reported by: <player / parent / coach>
### Body part and feeling (level 0-3, now vs history)
### What the team changes (monitor / reduce / pause)

## observer note (parent sideline)

- Evidence: observer (parent) - lower weight than the player's own words
- Match / opponent / score as seen:
### What the observer saw (facts only: scores, patterns, body language)
### Conditions or concerns (injury, heat, bad calls)
### Differences from the player's own account (fill in after the debrief)
